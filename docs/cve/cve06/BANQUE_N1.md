## M1 — Shapley value, general concept (market-standard, not KORA-specific)

1. In one sentence, what does a Shapley value distribute, and how?
   (The total value/payoff generated by a coalition, distributed among
   its members based on their average marginal contribution across
   all possible orderings of joining the coalition)
2. Name one standard application area outside KORA where Shapley
   values are used in practice. (E.g. multi-touch marketing
   attribution, or feature-importance explainability in machine
   learning)
