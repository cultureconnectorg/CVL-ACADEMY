## Prerequisites

Candidate must already hold the FMS-03 certification (M07/M12/M14
competency verified via that certification record, not re-tested
here).

## Objectives

Mixing Engineer and Mastering Engineer are long-established, frequently
fully independent careers in the real industry (freelance mastering
houses, dedicated mix engineers who never produce) — not a mere deeper
reading of Music Production. This specialization formalizes what
FMS-03/M14 already frames as advanced:

- Multi-style mix decision-making (translating a mix across genre
  conventions without losing the source material's intent).
- Mastering-for-release discipline (loudness/format targets, playback
  system translation, delivery-format literacy).
- Freelance/mastering-for-hire professional practice (briefs from a
  producer/artist who is not the candidate, revision discipline, and
  the professional boundary between "final mix" and "master").

## Modules

1. **Multi-style mix translation** — anchored on FMS-03/M14, building
   the cross-genre decision-making M07/M12 do not require at generalist
   depth.
2. **Mastering-for-release discipline** — loudness/format/delivery
   literacy as a standalone competency, not folded into "mixing."
3. **Freelance mix/master practice** — receiving a brief from someone
   else, revision cycles, and the professional line between mixing and
   mastering as two distinct deliverables.

## Assessment

A mix/master review exercise: candidate receives a real (or
representative) multi-track session or a mixed stem and a delivery
brief, and must produce a documented decision log plus the resulting
artifact — graded against real mixing/mastering practice, never
against an invented "correct answer" this corpus fabricates.
