## Objectives

Audiovisual Production is a distinct profession from every one of the
6 creative FMS-01→06 métiers — it covers producing video/audiovisual
content (music videos, behind-the-scenes, promotional/campaign video)
as its own deliverable, not as an adjunct to music production.

- Pre-production planning for an audiovisual shoot (shot list,
  schedule, crew/equipment needs) distinct from a music session plan.
- Production execution (camera, lighting, on-set sound capture).
- Post-production literacy (edit, color, delivery formats) — explicitly
  cross-referenced with FMS-03 at the audio-mix boundary: this
  formation teaches audio *capture* on set, not audio *mixing/
  mastering*, which stays FMS-03/FMS-09 territory.

## Modules

1. **Pre-production for audiovisual** — shot list, schedule, crew and
   equipment planning for a video/audiovisual deliverable.
2. **Production execution** — camera, lighting, on-set sound capture
   fundamentals.
3. **Post-production & delivery** — edit/color/delivery-format
   literacy, with an explicit boundary note: audio mixing/mastering of
   captured audio is FMS-03/FMS-09 territory, not re-taught here.

## Assessment

A documented shoot-to-delivery exercise: candidate plans and
documents a short audiovisual production (real or simulated brief)
end-to-end, graded against real audiovisual production practice.
