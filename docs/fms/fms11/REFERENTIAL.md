## Objectives

A Creative Director bridging brand, video, and campaign visuals is a
recognized, separately-titled role distinct from "Artist Branding
specialist," who owns positioning/identity more than visual execution
across media:

- Directing visuals across multiple media (not just brand assets) —
  video, live/campaign visuals, cross-media consistency.
- Briefing and managing other creative specialists (photographers,
  videographers, designers) rather than executing every asset
  personally.
- Explicit boundary discipline: does not re-teach audiovisual technique
  (FMS-10's territory) or live/event staging (FMS-12's territory) —
  this formation directs and briefs, it does not itself teach camera/
  edit/grade or stage/lighting-cue craft.

## Modules

1. **Brand-visual-direction foundation** — anchored on FMS-04/M04,M09
   by reference, assumed prerequisite competency.
2. **Cross-media creative direction** — extending beyond brand assets
   into video and campaign visual direction, the genuinely new material
   this formation adds.
3. **Briefing & managing creative specialists** — the management/
   delegation competency distinct from personally executing every
   asset; explicit cross-reference boundary against FMS-10 (audiovisual
   technique) and FMS-12 (live/event staging), never assumed as
   automatic overlap without direct candidate-portfolio evidence.

## Assessment

A creative-direction brief exercise: candidate produces a cross-media
creative direction package (brand + video + campaign visual direction
notes) and a specialist-briefing document, graded against real
creative-direction practice — never assuming, without evidence, that
the candidate can also direct a live show (that stays FMS-12's own
assessment).
