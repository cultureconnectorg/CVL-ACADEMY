## Objectives

Live/event show direction (staging, lighting, live visual cues) is
recognized as related to but distinct from brand/video creative
direction (FMS-11) — some professionals cross over, many do not. This
formation never assumes, without direct candidate-portfolio evidence,
that an FMS-11 holder can also direct a live show.

- Event/show staging fundamentals (stage layout, blocking, run-of-show
  literacy).
- Lighting and live visual cue direction (cue-calling literacy,
  coordinating with lighting/visual technicians during a live event).
- Live-event risk/contingency awareness (what changes when a plan meets
  a live audience and live technical conditions, distinct from a
  studio/pre-recorded creative-direction context).

## Modules

1. **Event/show staging fundamentals** — stage layout, blocking,
   run-of-show literacy.
2. **Lighting & live visual cue direction** — cue-calling and
   coordination with live technical crews.
3. **Live-event risk & contingency practice** — explicit cross-
   reference to FMS-11's shared boundary: this formation never assumes
   FMS-11 competency implies live-show direction competency, and vice
   versa.

## Assessment

A run-of-show direction exercise: candidate produces a staging/
lighting/cue plan for a representative live event brief, graded
against real live-production practice.
