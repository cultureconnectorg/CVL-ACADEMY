## Objectives

A&R and Talent Scouting is the professional function of *finding* an
artist before any of FMS-01→06's development/business/production/
branding/management/executive work begins on them — a genuinely
distinct entry point in the artist lifecycle:

- Talent identification (evaluating unsigned/early-stage artists
  against market and creative criteria).
- Sourcing and outreach practice (where and how real A&R work
  discovers talent, first-contact professional practice).
- Handoff literacy: what an A&R professional delivers to the FMS-01
  (Artist Development) stage once an artist is signed/onboarded —
  explicit boundary so this formation does not drift into teaching
  FMS-01's own development competencies.

## Modules

1. **Talent identification & evaluation** — criteria-based evaluation
   of early-stage/unsigned artists.
2. **Sourcing & outreach practice** — real-world discovery channels and
   first-contact professional practice.
3. **Signing handoff literacy** — what is delivered to FMS-01 (Artist
   Development) at handoff; explicit boundary against re-teaching
   FMS-01's own competencies.

## Assessment

A scouting-to-handoff exercise: candidate evaluates a representative
early-stage artist profile/brief, documents the evaluation, and
produces a signing-handoff package, graded against real A&R practice.
