## Objectives

A candidate who completes FRK-33 can design real technical creative-
lifecycle tracking, kept strictly separate from Fondation Cœurvolan's
rights/consent doctrine:

- Explain what technical creative-lifecycle tracking is (versioning,
  provenance over time) and why it stays distinct from Fondation
  Cœurvolan's memory/heritage doctrine, even though both touch
  "cultural memory": tracking answers "which version, when, derived
  from what"; Fondation Cœurvolan answers "who has the right, under
  what conditions" — questions of a fundamentally different nature.
- Explain why FREK owns technical tracking (versions, timestamps,
  derivation) while Fondation Cœurvolan owns community-rights and
  consent-governance doctrine: FREK is a technical traceability
  system; Fondation Cœurvolan is a rights-and-consent governance
  framework — non-interchangeable competencies, not just a
  convenient division of labor.
- Explain precisely why merging the two domains would be a design
  error, even though both concern "cultural memory": merging would
  make the technical system carry governance decisions it has no
  legitimacy to make, and conversely would dilute the doctrine into
  implementation details it should stay independent of.
- Design a real technical version/provenance chain for a creative
  work: distinct states over time (e.g. draft → published version →
  derivative), each with its own identifier referencing its
  predecessor — entirely without touching any question of who may use,
  access, or claim rights over the work.
- Keep the boundary absolute: a technical schema is complete and valid
  on its own even if it never mentions Fondation Cœurvolan's
  consent doctrine at all — that is the proof the two domains are
  genuinely independent, not merely decoupled by convention.

## Modules

1. **Creative-lifecycle tracking fundamentals** — the technical-vs-

   vs. who-has-the-right).

   (draft → published → derivative) with predecessor-referencing
   identifiers, entirely rights-free.
3. **Boundary discipline vs. Fondation Cœurvolan** — the doctrine is
   referenced only as a boundary, never duplicated or merged; a valid
   technical schema needs zero mention of it.
